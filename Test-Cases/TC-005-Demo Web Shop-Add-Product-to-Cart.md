Test Case ID: TC-005

Short Summary: Verify that a product can be added to the Shopping Cart.

Environment: Google Chrome, Windows 11

Preconditions:
User is on the Product Details page of the 14.1-inch Laptop.

Steps:
1. Open the Product Details page of the 14.1-inch Laptop.
2. Enter quantity 1 in the Qty field.
3. Click Add to cart.
4. Open the Shopping Cart.

Expected Result:
The 14.1-inch Laptop is added to the Shopping Cart with quantity 1 and the correct price is displayed.

Actual Result:
The 14.1-inch Laptop was added to the Shopping Cart with quantity 1 and the price was displayed correctly.

Status: Pass
